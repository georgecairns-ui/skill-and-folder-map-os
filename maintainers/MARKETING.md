# Marketing notes: Skill and Folder Map OS

## In one sentence

See every skill and every folder you have, mapped as 2 glowing maps, and what to tidy first.

## Before and after, for the owner

**Before:** you've asked Claude to build you a skill here and there, maybe a few plugins got installed along the way, and nobody remembers what's actually there any more. Some skills share a name without anyone noticing. Meanwhile your Documents, Desktop and Downloads are full of duplicates, old downloads, final-final versions and folders nobody's opened in years, and nobody has the hour it would take to find out what's actually in there.

**After:** open the Skills map and see every skill at a glance, Claude in the middle, grouped by what they do for the business. Switch to the Files map and see the same folders you look at every day, drawn as a map you can click into, down to a single file, with a Clean-up page that lists the duplicates, the very big files, the old downloads and the untidy names, biggest first. Claude can suggest what to merge, retire, rename or tidy, and the person decides.

Time saved: to check. Do not quote a figure until it has been measured with real users.

## 3 things to show in a 60-second video

1. **The glowing map growing in.** A fresh scan, and the dots and hubs build outward from the centre on the dark, glowing stage.
2. **Clicking down through folders to a single file.** Click a hub to go into a folder, again to go deeper, then click a file to see its folder, size and last change.
3. **The Clean-up page.** The list of what needs tidying, biggest first, each with "See them" and "Find on the map".
