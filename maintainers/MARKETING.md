# Marketing notes: Skill Map OS

## In one sentence

Skill Map OS finds every skill and command Claude can use on your computer and draws them as one map, so you can see what's already covered, what's doubled up, and what still needs a skill.

## Before and after, for the owner

**Before:** you've asked Claude to build you a skill here and there, maybe a few plugins got installed along the way, and nobody remembers what's actually there any more. Some skills share a name without anyone noticing. Some haven't been touched in months. You've no idea which parts of the business actually have Claude doing something, and which don't.

**After:** open the map and see every skill at a glance, Claude in the middle, grouped by what they do for the business. Click any dot to see what it does and how often it's been used lately. The Gaps page shows exactly which parts of the business have nothing set up yet, with ideas for what a skill there could do. Claude can suggest what to merge, retire or update, and the person decides.

Time saved: to check. Do not quote a figure until it has been measured with real users.

## 3 things to show in a 60-second video

1. **The map growing in.** A fresh scan, and the dots and hubs build outward from Claude in the middle.
2. **Clicking a hub and a dot.** Click a hub to see everything in that group; click a dot to see one skill's details and how much it's been used.
3. **The Gaps page with an empty area.** A business area with "Nothing here yet" and a few ideas for what a skill could do there, ending with the "Want these built for you?" button.
